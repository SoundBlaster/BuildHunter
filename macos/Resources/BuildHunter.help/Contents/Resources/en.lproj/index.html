BuildHunter Help

Find build outputs and caches, explore their sizes, and create your own scan rules.

  • Scan a folder
  • Explore results
  • Choose scan rules
  • Write a scan template

Scan a folder

  1. Choose File → Open Folder… and select a folder.
  2. Wait for the scan or watch results arrive. BuildHunter reads files and folders; it does not delete artifacts.
  3. Check scan warnings for locations that could not be fully read.

Explore results

Sort the results by size and open an item in Finder. Use the diagram to explore the distribution of artifacts within folders. Select Rescan to refresh the report.

Choose scan rules

Open BuildHunter → Settings → Scan Rules to choose what appears in future scans. Swift, Rust, and Python rules and your own JSON templates are free. The Mac App Store edition may also offer an optional Catalog of additional rules. Changes affect new scans; existing reports retain their results.
